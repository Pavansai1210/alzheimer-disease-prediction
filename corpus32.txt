Common Cold Overview
The common cold is a viral infection of your nose and throat (upper respiratory tract). It's usually harmless, although it might not feel that way. Many types of viruses can cause a common cold. Healthy adults can expect to have two or three colds each year. Most people recover from a common cold in a week or 10 days. Symptoms might last longer in people who smoke.

Symptoms of Common Cold
- Runny or stuffy nose
- Sore throat and cough
- Congestion
- Slight body aches or headache
- Sneezing
- Low-grade fever
- Malaise (generally feeling unwell)

Medicines for Common Cold
- Pain relievers (Acetaminophen, Ibuprofen)
- Decongestant nasal sprays
- Cough syrups
- Antihistamines
- Vitamin C and Zinc supplements

Precautions and Management
- Wash hands thoroughly and frequently
- Disinfect touched surfaces
- Cover your mouth when sneezing or coughing
- Avoid sharing utensils or towels
- Stay hydrated and get plenty of rest
