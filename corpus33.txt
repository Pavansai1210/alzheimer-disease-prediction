Constipation Overview
Constipation is a condition in which you have fewer than three bowel movements a week, or your bowel movements are hard, dry, and difficult to pass. It is a very common problem. While it's usually not serious, it can be uncomfortable and may lead to other issues if left untreated. Chronic constipation can interfere with daily tasks and quality of life.

Symptoms of Constipation
- Fewer than three bowel movements per week
- Passing lumpy or hard stools
- Straining to have bowel movements
- Feeling as though there's a blockage
- Feeling as though you can't completely empty your bowels

Medicines for Constipation
- Fiber supplements (Psyllium)
- Stimulant laxatives (Bisacodyl)
- Osmotic laxatives (Polyethylene glycol)
- Stool softeners (Docusate)
- Lubricant laxatives (Mineral oil)

Precautions and Management
- Eat high-fiber foods (beans, vegetables, whole grains)
- Drink plenty of water throughout the day
- Stay active and exercise regularly
- Don't ignore the urge to have a bowel movement
- Manage stress and set a regular schedule
