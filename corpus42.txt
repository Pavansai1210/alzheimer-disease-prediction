Psoriasis Overview
Psoriasis is a skin disease that causes a rash with itchy, scaly patches, most commonly on the knees, elbows, trunk and scalp. Psoriasis is a common, long-term (chronic) disease with no cure. It can be painful, interfere with sleep and make it hard to concentrate. The condition tends to go through cycles, flaring for a few weeks or months, then subsiding for a while or going into remission.

Symptoms of Psoriasis
- A patchy rash that ranges from small spots to large eruptions
- Rashes that vary in color (pink, red, purple or brown)
- Small scaling spots (commonly seen in children)
- Dry, cracked skin that may bleed
- Itching, burning or soreness
- Cyclic flares that last for weeks or months

Medicines for Psoriasis
- Topical corticosteroids
- Vitamin D analogues
- Retinoids
- Salicylic acid
- Coal tar
- Phototherapy (light therapy)

Precautions and Management
- Use moisturizing lotions regularly
- Avoid skin injuries (cuts, scrapes)
- Avoid triggers like stress and smoking
- Take daily baths to remove scales
- Spend a small amount of time in natural sunlight
- Avoid heavy alcohol consumption
