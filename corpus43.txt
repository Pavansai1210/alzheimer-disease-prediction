Ringworm Overview
Ringworm is a common skin infection that is caused by a fungus. It’s called “ringworm” because it can cause a circular rash (shaped like a ring) that is usually red and itchy. Anyone can get ringworm. The fungi that cause this infection can live on skin, surfaces, and on household items like clothing, towels, and brushes. Ringworm can be spread between people, animals and objects.

Symptoms of Ringworm
- A scaly, ring-shaped area, typically on the trunk or limbs
- Itchiness
- A clear or scaly area inside the ring
- Slightly raised, expanding rings
- A round, flat patch of itchy skin

Medicines for Ringworm
- Antifungal creams (Clotrimazole, Terbinafine)
- Antifungal powders
- Oral antifungal medications (Griseofulvin for scalp)
- Anti-fungal shampoos

Precautions and Management
- Keep your skin clean and dry
- Wear shoes that allow air to circulate around feet
- Don't share personal items (towels, hairbrushes)
- Wash hands after playing with pets
- Avoid touching the infected area to prevent spreading
