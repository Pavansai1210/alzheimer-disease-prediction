Vertigo Overview
Vertigo is a sensation of feeling off balance. If you have these dizzy spells, you might feel like you are spinning or that the world around you is spinning. Vertigo is often caused by an inner ear problem. It is a symptom of several different conditions and can be mild or severe. It can happen at any age, but it's more common in people aged 65 or older.

Symptoms of Vertigo
- Spinning sensation
- Tilting or swaying feeling
- Unbalanced or pulled in one direction
- Nausea and vomiting
- Abnormal or jerking eye movements (nystagmus)
- Headache and sweating
- Ringing in the ears (tinnitus) or hearing loss

Medicines for Vertigo
- Meclizine (Antivert)
- Diazepam (Valium)
- Prochlorperazine
- Antihistamines (Benadryl)
- Scopolamine patches

Precautions and Management
- Move the head slowly during daily activities
- Sit down immediately when feeling dizzy
- Use a cane for walking if you're at risk of falling
- Avoid bright lights and rapid movements
- Manage stress and get enough sleep
- Perform Epley maneuver (if recommended for BPPV)
