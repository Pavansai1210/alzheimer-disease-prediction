
Fever is a temporary increase in body temperature, often associated with an infection.

Common symptoms:
- High body temperature
- Chills and shivering
- Sweating
- Headache
- Muscle aches
- Weakness
- Loss of appetite

Possible causes:
- Viral infections
- Bacterial infections
- Other inflammatory conditions

General precautions:
- Drink sufficient fluids.
- Get adequate rest.
- Wear lightweight clothing.
- Monitor your body temperature.
- Consult a healthcare professional if symptoms persist or worsen.

Medical advice:
Fever can have different causes. Consult a qualified doctor for diagnosis and appropriate treatment. Seek urgent medical care if you experience difficulty breathing, confusion, seizures, or other severe symptoms.
